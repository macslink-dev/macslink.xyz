// MacsLink Slop Chest · Windows & Android

The slop chest,
balanced to the cent

Keep the ship's store without the bond-book headache: stock in, sales out, every crew member's balance, and a clean statement at the end of the month. Simple, offline, and built for how the slop chest actually runs.

📦

Stock & prices

Hold your item list with prices and quantities. Receipts in, sales out — the balance on hand is always right.

🧾

Crew accounts

Each purchase goes on the crew member's account. See every balance at a glance, settle at pay-off or month end.

📊

Monthly statement

One click produces the slop-chest statement and each person's slip — ready for signature and the accounts.

💱

Your currency

Works in the currency your company uses for the bond, with clean rounding and no surprises in the totals.

🔒

Offline & private

Runs on the ship's PC or a phone. Crew and sales data stay on board — no cloud, no account.

📱

Windows & Android

Keep it on the office PC or carry it to the store. Same stock, same balances.
