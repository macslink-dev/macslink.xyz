// Why MacsLink

Built on watch,
not in an office

Every app here started as a job that wasted time on board. They are shaped by how the work actually runs at sea.

Offline by design

Everything runs on the ship's own PC or phone. No connection needed, and no manifest, ballast or inspection data ever leaves the vessel.

One job, done well

No bloated all-in-one. Each app is small, fast and focused on a single part of the deck officer's day.

Your formats

MACS3 cXML, your Tank-per-tank log, the port's own report forms — MacsLink fits into the files you already use, not the other way around.

Fair pricing

Free where it can be, a low one-month licence where it can't. No subscriptions you forget to cancel, no per-seat traps.
