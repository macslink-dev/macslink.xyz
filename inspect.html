// MacsLink Inspect · Windows & Android

Rounds & inspections,
without the clipboard

Walk the deck, tick the items, snap the photos — MacsLink Inspect keeps every round in order and turns it into a clean report in one tap. Built for the jobs the mate signs off every week. Free.

🧭

By category

Mooring ropes, winches, cranes, holds, hatch covers, tanks & spaces, pilot ladders, gangway — each as its own checklist, ready on the phone or the ship's PC.

📷

Findings & photos

Mark each item, add a note and attach photos straight from the camera. Deficiencies stand out so nothing is missed at the next round.

📄

One-tap report

Export a clean, dated inspection report to hand over, file in the SMS or send ashore — no retyping.

🕓

History per item

See when each item was last inspected and what was found, so overdue rounds are obvious at a glance.

🔒

Stays on board

Everything is stored on your own device. No account, no cloud — inspection data never leaves the vessel.

📱

Windows & Android

Use it on the bridge PC or in your pocket on rounds. Same checklists, same reports.
