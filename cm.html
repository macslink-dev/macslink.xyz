MacsLink CM
Container Manager

Follow discharge and loading on your phone, bay by bay, straight from the BAPLIE plan — instead of printed bay plans and a pen.

  • Graphic bay plan from the arrival and departure BAPLIE (SMDG 3.1 and 2.2) — tap or swipe to mark containers discharged / loaded.
  • Restows shown in both plans, marked with a circle, with from → to positions.
  • DG checks — container no., UN no., IMDG class, marine pollutant, limited quantity.
  • Reefers — plug / unplug tracking and a reefer report with open issues.
  • Cranes & time left — cranes per bay, productivity, moves remaining and estimated finish.
  • Reports — progress, watch handover, DG, reefers, lashing; share by e-mail or message.
  • Watch handover between officers' phones and merging of markings (officer + AB).
  • Offline — everything stays on the phone; no account, no server, no ads.
